## What is new in 1.5.0

Bigger complication icons, and the step-count shoe is redrawn at its real size so it no longer looks stretched.

Step counts keep three significant digits (3,88 or 10,1), so they always fit under the icon.

Atkinson Hyperlegible Next joins the clock font choices, and the weekday word and the calendar number follow whichever font you pick.

The heart rate now appears as soon as the sensor reports a new reading, instead of waiting for the next minute.

The weekday word is slightly larger with even strokes, so the A has two equal legs.

The settings page shows the installed version.

Under the hood: drawing no longer crashes if the watch is low on memory, and the face asks for far less memory for phone messages.

There is also a new purple and white dusk theme in the store screenshots.
